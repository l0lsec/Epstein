    About This Archive

    Purpose

    This platform provides public access to Epstein Library documents related to the Jeffrey Epstein case, including court records, DOJ disclosures, flight logs, and other official documents. Our goal is to make this information searchable and accessible for researchers, journalists, and the public for easier analysis and research that will lead to more revelations and justice.

    Document Sources

    All documents in this archive were downloaded directly from the U.S. Department of Justice website. These materials were publicly released as part of official disclosures related to the Jeffrey Epstein case.

    ⚠️
    Important Note on Redactions:

    The vast majority of documents in this archive are heavily redacted, with large portions of text blacked out. This makes many documents difficult or impossible to comprehend in their entirety. The redactions were applied by government agencies prior to public release. We present the documents exactly as they were released by the DOJ.

    The archive includes court records, evidence files, correspondence, flight logs, contact books, OIG reports, and other official documents from the Epstein Library.

    Technology

    This platform uses advanced search technology including:

    • Full-text search across all extracted document content
    • Semantic AI search for finding conceptually related documents
    • LLM-powered Q&A for intelligent document analysis
